Breaker Bay

Three directions for the page, built on the real Standard pool and the curated cost model from stage 1. Every number is live; try them on a phone too.

Proposals

  1. A · Workbench: the spec as written. A card settings column (a drawer on a phone), one table of ice × breakers with type toggles and a heatmap, a cost breakdown on tap, the box plot under it.
  2. B · Breaker cards: one card per breaker with its median, spread and reach, its own sliders and variant switches on the card, and every matching piece of ice a tap away. Browses well on a phone.
  3. C · Run planner: stack the ice of a server and rank rigs of up to three breakers by the credits for one run through it, counters spent along the way. Answers “what gets me into this server”.
