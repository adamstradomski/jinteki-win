jinteki.win

Tools for Netrunner players: look back at a game you just played, or see what the tournament meta is doing, or follow a tournament from Discord.

Trace

Paste a finished jinteki.net game log, or send it over with the bookmarklet, to get turn-by-turn charts, credit and click usage, and a per-card cost breakdown. Logs are parsed in your browser.

Open Trace

Market Research Beta

Card meta from NSG Netrunner Standard tournaments: the most played cards and identities, trends over time, winrates, and what top-cut decks play.

Open Market Research

Breaker Bay Beta

What it costs to break every piece of Standard ice with every icebreaker, and which rig of breakers gets you into a server for the fewest credits. Counters, variants and support cards are yours to set.

Open Breaker Bay

Cobra Bot

A Discord bot for Cobra tournaments: post standings, pairings, the top cut and its bracket, and player results to your channel with /cobra, straight from tournaments.nullsignal.games.

Open Cobra Bot
